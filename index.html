Privacy Policy & Security Standards

Gullak AI • Transparent, Privacy-First Architecture

Zero-Server, 100% Client-Side Private Gullak AI does not maintain user databases or track personal spending. All transactions, monthly allowances, and peer debts live solely inside your browser's private localStorage.

1. Where Is Your Data Stored?

Your financial records (monthly allowance, spending logs, peer debts, and categories) are saved directly in your web browser's localStorage. No financial data is ever collected, sold, or shared with third parties or advertisers.

2. UPI & Bank SMS Processing

When you paste SMS debit alerts or use the PhonePe sync hub, our heuristic regex engine executes entirely on your local CPU. The message text is parsed in memory to extract vendor and amount details and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Google Gemini AI Copilot Integration

If you optionally provide your own Google Gemini API key:

  • Your API key is stored securely in your browser's local settings.
  • Questions asked to the Copilot or receipts submitted for OCR are transmitted directly over encrypted HTTPS (SSL) between your device and Google's official Gemini endpoint (generativelanguage.googleapis.com).
  • If no API key is supplied, Gullak AI automatically runs in complete offline rule-based mode.

4. Complete Data Ownership & Erasure

You retain 100% ownership of your data at all times. You can export complete backups anytime in JSON or CSV, or permanently erase all stored records by clicking "Logout / Exit" or clearing your browser's cache.

Personal Financial Management Tool By using Gullak AI, you acknowledge and agree to the guidelines and disclaimers outlined below.

1. Non-Financial Advice Disclaimer

Gullak AI is an informational self-help budgeting and expense tracking utility. The AI recommendations, budget forecasts, and "Can I Afford This?" simulations are automated estimates and do not constitute certified financial, tax, or legal advice.

2. Peer Debts & Social Lending

The peer debts tracker is a personal record-keeping ledger for informal loans with friends and family. Gullak AI is not a payment gateway, escrow service, or financial intermediary. Settle and reminder actions reflect your personal notes only.

3. User Responsibility & Backups

Because your data is stored locally on your device, clearing browser history or switching private/incognito tabs can erase un-backed data. We strongly recommend using the Backup Data (JSON) feature periodically to retain your records.
